01Training

What we coach

Four kinds of sessions. All of them 1‑on‑1 with the same coach, from the first rep to the last.

  • 01

    Strength & conditioning

    Speed, power and strength work planned around your season and your match days.

  • 02

    Rehab after ACL surgery

    Strength work after a reconstruction, until the knee is ready for your sport again.

  • 03

    Injury rehab

    The same 1‑on‑1 sessions after other sports injuries and operations.

  • 04

    1‑on‑1 personal training

    For anyone who wants a coach's full attention, athlete or not.

  3. 03Return to running and sport
